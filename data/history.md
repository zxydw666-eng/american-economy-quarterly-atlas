# Historical boundaries

All intervals include the start date and exclude the end date. Presidential bands represent continuous administrations. The two nonconsecutive Trump administrations are distinct. Truman's tenure began before the economic series window. The current administration has an open end.

Dates use the constitutional start of the term, including when the public ceremony occurred the next day. The Kennedy–Johnson transition occurred on November 22, 1963, and the Nixon–Ford transition on August 9, 1974. Sources include the [Library of Congress terms of office](https://loc.gov/exhibits/inaugural/terms.html), its [presidential inaugurations resource guide](https://guides.loc.gov/presidential-inaugurations), and [USAGov's presidential office record](https://www.usa.gov/presidents).

Contraction bands begin on the first day of the month after the NBER peak and end on the first day of the month after the trough. For example, the February 2020 peak and April 2020 trough shade March and April. These are monthly classifications even when the economic observations are quarterly. See the [NBER chronology](https://www.nber.org/research/data/us-business-cycle-expansions-and-contractions) and [dating conventions](https://www.nber.org/research/business-cycle-dating).
