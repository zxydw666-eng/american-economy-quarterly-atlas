Each quarter belongs to the president serving on its final day. Transition quarters can span administrations. The comparison includes only observed quarters within the selected window. Truman’s coverage starts in 1948 and the current administration’s coverage ends with the latest available quarter.

These descriptive comparisons cannot isolate policy effects. Presidents inherit economic conditions, monetary policy is set independently, and shocks cross term boundaries. Historical observations can be revised. In 2025 Q4, missing October observations leave the unemployment and CPI aggregates blank.
